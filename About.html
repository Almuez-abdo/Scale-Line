About Scale Line

Our Story

Engineering Excellence Since 2017

Scale Line is an engineering company founded in 2017. It employs a number of architects, civil engineers and interior engineers, and is one of the leading companies in the field of reconstruction and construction in Sudan.

Our goal is not to be just another engineering company, but to master our specialization in order to achieve comprehensive development. As a leading company we work in development, project management, architectural and interior design, building implementation and finishing, feasibility studies, quantity surveying and full project costing, supervision, arbitration, value engineering and training.

We aspire to occupy a leading position in every engineering field, delivering projects with a high level of quality and performance. To achieve this vision, the company relies on qualified professional technical cadres with the financial and technical capability to execute major projects on schedule.
